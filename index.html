// 05 ARCHIVE & FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS ABOUT KAIZENHACKS

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

A quick overview of who we are, what we run, and how you can get involved.

KaizenHacks is a student-led hackathon and innovation organization. We help students create, organize, and participate in hackathons, ideathons, competitions, workshops, and other technology-driven events. We're intentionally starting small and building our identity through real student-led initiatives.
Get involved by joining the community, taking part in events as they're announced, or helping organize what comes next. Whether you build, design, organize, or mentor — there's a place for you. Drop your details through the form and we'll be in touch as things take shape.
Hackathons, ideathons, coding competitions, workshops, and other technology-driven events — always built around continuous improvement. Our first events are currently in the works, and every one of them will be better than the last.
Kaizen means continuous improvement. Every event should be better than the previous one. Every project should teach something. Every failure should become feedback. Every student should have an opportunity to build something they can be proud of. Build. Improve. Repeat.
