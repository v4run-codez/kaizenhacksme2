// 05 ARCHIVE & FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS SPECIFICATIONS

SYSTEM SPECIFICATIONS & CLARIFICATIONS

Key guidelines on application screening, hardware support, and team formats.

We review technical problem-solving ability, past prototypes, and clarity of thought regarding UN Sustainable Development Goals. No previous hackathon victory required — beginners with genuine ambition and systems curiosity are warmly encouraged.
Teams can consist of 1 to 4 individuals. You can apply as a pre-formed team or apply solo and connect with other builders during the on-site check-in mixer.
Bring your laptop, chargers, any hardware/microcontrollers (ESP32, Raspberry Pi, sensors) you wish to experiment with, and a valid photo ID. Fast Wi-Fi, power extensions, and refreshments are fully provided.
You and your team retain 100% ownership of your IP. KAIZEN HACKS and partners take zero equity.
